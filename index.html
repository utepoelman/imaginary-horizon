THE MISSION: Navigate Home from the Fourth Dimension

  • Your ship is lost in a multi-dimensional nebula. To get home, you must be first to reach the wormhole by calculating jumps through "Complex Space."
  • Mathematical Context:
    Adding is a "Thruster" (linear movement).
    Multiplying is a "Warp Turn" (multiplying by complex numbers involves rotation and scaling around the origin).
    Complex conjugating is a "Reality Mirror" (reflection across the real axis).
  • Ships start at random positions within the sector (-7 to 7 on both axes)
  • Only maneuvres that keep you in the sector are allowed
  • Click RECALIBRATE to reset all navigation systems (replaces all systems, but skips your turn)

QUICK CONTROLS & TIPS

  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Press 1-8 to use corresponding card
  • Thruster Burn (Addition): Green cards move your ship directly
  • Warp Rotation (Multiplication): Blue cards rotate and scale your position
  • Special Cards: Orange cards have unique effects
  • Card Cycling: After using a card, it's replaced with a new one
  • Recalibration: Use the orange RECALIBRATE card to refresh your hand (skips turn)
  • Wormhole Shift: Rare special card moves the target wormhole
  • Reality Mirror: Flips your position across the real axis
